<why>
Patch core/0082 (now in patches/llama/core/) turns on "prompt offload": with weights on the host (-ngl 0), large prompt batches run their mat-muls on a GPU from copies of the weights, guarded by a cap on the GPU's compute buffer (TOSH_OFFLOAD_MAX_COMPUTE_MB, default 1536; the log says "op offload on: ..." or "op offload off: ... would be ..."). On the dense gemma4 12B it raised prompt speed from 12.9 to 21.3 tok/s with byte-identical text. MoE models use MUL_MAT_ID; whether offload is correct and pays there is unknown. This ticket measures it on the 35B MoE.
</why>
