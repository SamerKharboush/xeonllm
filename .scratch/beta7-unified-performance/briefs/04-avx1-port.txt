<why>
The target CPU is an Intel Xeon E5-2697 v2 (Ivy Bridge, 12 cores): AVX and F16C, NO AVX2, NO FMA, no BMI2. The fork's patch 0022 added a multi-row dot, `ggml_get_vec_dot_multi(type)` in ggml/src/ggml-cpu/arch/x86/quants.c: one quantized weight block is unpacked once and applied to 1-4 activation rows (the tokens routed to one MoE expert in a decode or a speculative verify window). Its caller is the expert mat-mul in ggml/src/ggml-cpu/ggml-cpu.c (`multi_cache`, around line 1571; `GGML_CPU_NO_MULTI_DOT=1` disables it). Every x86 implementation, and the getter itself, sit inside `#if defined(__AVX2__)`, and the getter returns NULL otherwise. So on this Xeon the multi-row path never runs. Measured on the host: verifying k tokens of the 35B MoE costs t(2)=1.42x, t(4)=2.16x, t(8)=5.91x of one token; sharing the unpack across rows can only lower those.
</why>
