<facts> (measured by the orchestrator on 2026-11-02; use them, do not invent others)
- Hardware: Mac Pro 2013, 2x FirePro D700, recommendedMaxWorkingSetSize 6.44 GB each, 64 GB RAM.
- The app launches Qwen3-14B-Q4_K_M (about 9 GB of weights) split by layer over both cards with
  -ngl 99 -c 32768 and an f16 KV cache. Per card that is about 6.9 GB (MTL0: 3954 MiB weights +
  ~2688 MiB KV + 284 MiB compute), above 6.44 GB, so the driver pages. Tensor split is ~7.1 GB/card.
- Paged, a long prefill command buffer hit the GPU watchdog (kIOAccelCommandBufferCallbackErrorTimeout)
  and every later request got HTTP 500 "Compute error.". Patch metal/0078 (beta.6) cuts prefill
  graphs into shorter command buffers and the same request now passes; the overcommit itself remains.
- -ub 256 or -c 8192 also avoided the failure on the old engine.
- Because the app passes -ngl 99, the engine logs "not fitting params to free device memory:
  n_gpu_layers already set by user to 99, abort" and its own --fit never runs.
</facts>
